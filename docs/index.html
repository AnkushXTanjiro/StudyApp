COMMON QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

Does StudyApp work offline without internet?

Yes! All 2,561+ built-in questions, lab vivas, your BEU syllabus, GEC Sheikhpura CSE-A timetable, and offline marking-scheme grading work completely offline. Internet is only needed for Google Sign-In, cloud leaderboard sync, and Makina's Gemini AI voice/explanations (offline Android TTS is also built in as a fallback).

Why does Android ask to "Allow from this source" when installing?

Android shows that standard security prompt for any direct .apk file installed outside Google Play. Once you allow your browser or file manager once, installation finishes normally.

When does Makina explain test questions?

Makina greets you when you arrive, gives an optional app tour, and stays out of your way during exams. After submitting a test, tap Explain with AI on any specific question you didn't understand — you can even tap the microphone to ask follow-up questions verbally.
